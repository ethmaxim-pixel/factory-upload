Factory Upload

Завод загрузка роликов

Uses YouTube API Services · Privacy Policy

Factory Upload is a private publishing tool of a small video studio. It uploads short vertical videos produced by the studio to the studio's own YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public and has no users other than the owner of these channels.

How it works

  1. The channel owner connects each of their YouTube channels once, through the standard Google sign-in and consent screen.
  2. When the owner approves a finished video in the studio's internal tool, Factory Upload uploads it to the chosen channel with the title, description and tags written by the owner.
  3. Factory Upload reads the basic channel information (channel ID and title) to confirm which channel is connected, and checks the processing status of the videos it uploaded.

YouTube API Services

Factory Upload uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy and our Privacy Policy.
