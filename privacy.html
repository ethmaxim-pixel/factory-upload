Privacy Policy

Factory Upload · effective 1 November 2026

Factory Upload is a private tool that uploads videos produced by a small studio to the studio's own YouTube channels. Its only user is the owner of those channels. This policy explains what data the tool accesses and how it is handled.

YouTube API Services

Factory Upload uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Data we access

  • youtube.upload — to upload the owner's videos to the connected channel and set their thumbnails.
  • youtube.readonly — to read the connected channel's ID and title and the processing status of the videos Factory Upload uploaded.

We do not access comments, subscribers, analytics, other people's channels or any other account data.

How we use, store and share it

  • The OAuth access token for each connected channel, the channel ID and title, and the IDs of the videos we uploaded are stored on the owner's private server with access restricted to the owner.
  • This data is used only to upload the owner's videos and to confirm that they were published.
  • We do not sell, share or disclose this data to third parties, and do not use it for advertising.
  • Neither this website nor the tool uses cookies or stores any information on your device.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept only while a channel stays connected. Data obtained from YouTube API Services is refreshed or deleted within 30 days. When access is revoked, the stored token and channel data are deleted within 7 days.

You can revoke Factory Upload's access at any time on the Google security settings page.
